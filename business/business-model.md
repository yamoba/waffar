# Waffar Business Model

## Revenue Streams

### 1. Affiliate Commissions (Primary — 60% of revenue)
- Earn 2-8% commission on every purchase redirected to partner stores
- Amazon Associates Egypt: ~4% average
- Noon Affiliate: ~5% average
- Jumia KOL: ~6% average
- B.Tech / 2B: Direct partnership negotiations, target 3-5%
- Estimated RPM: 15-25 EGP per 1000 clicks

### 2. Pro Subscriptions (25% of revenue)
- **Free tier**: 5 tracked products, 3 alerts, basic search
- **Pro tier (49 EGP/month)**: Unlimited tracking, unlimited alerts, AI advisor, budget tools, priority notifications, ad-free
- **Premium tier (99 EGP/month)**: Everything in Pro + API access, family sharing (5 accounts), receipt analyzer, cashback tracking
- Target: 5% free-to-paid conversion at scale

### 3. Promoted Listings & Sponsored Placements (10% of revenue)
- Stores can pay to boost visibility in search results and comparison tables
- Sponsored "Deal of the Day" slots
- Featured store badges
- CPM model: 30-80 EGP per 1000 impressions

### 4. Data & Insights (5% of revenue, Phase 2)
- Aggregated, anonymized market intelligence reports sold to retailers
- Price positioning analysis for brands
- Consumer demand signals
- Category trend reports

## Cost Structure

### Fixed Costs (Monthly)
- Infrastructure (servers, DB, CDN): 3,000-8,000 EGP
- Domain, SSL, monitoring tools: 500 EGP
- AI model hosting: 2,000 EGP

### Variable Costs
- Proxy/scraping infrastructure: scales with product count
- Payment processing (Paymob): 2.5% + 2 EGP per transaction
- Email/SMS notifications: usage-based

### Team (at launch)
- 2 full-stack engineers
- 1 scraper/data engineer
- 1 product/growth person
- Monthly burn: ~80,000-120,000 EGP

## Go-To-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Launch (Month 1-3)
- Focus on electronics and smartphones (highest AOV, clearest comparison)
- SEO-first: product pages indexed for "[product name] سعر في مصر"
- Content marketing: buying guides, price drop alerts shared on social media
- Telegram/WhatsApp groups for deal alerts
- Target: 10,000 MAU, 500 tracked products

### Phase 2: Growth (Month 4-8)
- Expand to home appliances, gaming, fashion accessories
- Launch Pro subscription
- Browser extension for Chrome/Edge
- Influencer partnerships (tech YouTube channels in Egypt)
- Target: 50,000 MAU, 3,000 tracked products, 200 Pro subscribers

### Phase 3: Scale (Month 9-12)
- Expand store coverage to 15+ stores
- Launch mobile app (React Native)
- Community features (reviews, discussions, group buys)
- B2B data products
- Regional expansion exploration (Saudi Arabia, UAE)
- Target: 200,000 MAU, 10,000+ products, 1,500 Pro subscribers
